## Teaching assistance, ETH Zürich (2014–2018)

- Optical mineralogy and petrography: *Microscopy of magmatic rocks* (MSc, one semester), *Metamorphic microscopy* (MSc, two semesters) and *Mikroskopie der Gesteine* (BSc, one semester, taught in German).
- Volcanology field courses (MSc) on volcanic deposits, eruptive processes, igneous petrology and volcanic hazards in southern Italy (Aeolian Islands, Mt. Etna, Campi Flegrei, Vesuvius) and the Cape Verde archipelago.
- Field mapping of crystalline rocks (BSc): regional metamorphism in Val Piora, Ticino, and the Adamello pluton and its contact aureole.
